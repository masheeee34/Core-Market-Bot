# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about compatibility, security, and setup.

---

### Q1 : Are all products compatible with Windows 11 ?
**Yes**, our entire lineup is tested and compatible with Windows 10 (21H2, 22H2) and Windows 11 (22H2, 23H2, 24H2).

---

### Q2 : Does this work with both AMD and Intel CPUs ?
**Yes**, all hypervisors and loaders support Intel Core (i3, i5, i7, i9) and AMD Ryzen (3, 5, 7, 9) processors.

---

### Q3 : Antivirus flagged the loader upon download, what should I do ?
This is a standard false-positive caused by security obfuscation layers. Follow our [3. Security & Antivirus](../01-prerequisites/antivirus.md) guide to add an exclusion folder.

---

### Q4 : What payment methods are supported ?

* **Cryptocurrency :** Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), USDT.
* **Credit Card & PayPal :** Available via support ticket on our Discord server.
