# 🎫 Support & Discord Tickets

Our technical staff and 24/7 automated support engines are ready to assist you anytime.

---

### 📩 How to Open a Ticket
1. Join our Discord community: **[discord.gg/coremarket](https://discord.gg/coremarket)**
2. Go to the **`# 🎫・create-ticket`** channel.
3. Select the department for your inquiry:
   * **🛒 Orders & Payments :** Credit Card, PayPal, or Crypto help.
   * **🛠️ Technical Support :** Installation guidance or game configuration.
   * **🔑 Key Issues & Renewals :** Replacement or license top-ups.
4. A dedicated private channel will instantly be created with our team.
