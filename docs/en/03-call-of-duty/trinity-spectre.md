# 🔮 Trinity Spectre (Black Ops 6 / Warzone)

High-performance Ring-0 external software designed for Call of Duty: Black Ops 6 & Warzone.

---

### 🌟 Features
* **Hypervisor Ring-0 External :** Runs entirely outside game memory address spaces for maximum security.
* **100% Streamproof :** Completely invisible on OBS, Discord Screen Share, and recording software.
* **Advanced Visuals (ESP) :** 2D/3D boxes, skeleton rendering, health bars, weapon info, and distance.
* **Predictive Aimbot :** Full weapon recoil smoothing and bullet-drop velocity calculation.

---

### 🕹️ Default Keybindings
* **Main Menu :** `Insert` or `F11`
* **Panic Key (Emergency Exit) :** `Delete`
