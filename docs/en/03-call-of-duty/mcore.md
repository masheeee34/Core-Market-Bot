# 🎯 M-Core External (BO7 / Warzone)

M-Core provides a fluid, high-framerate kernel overlay designed for competitive Warzone and Multiplayer matches.

---

### 🛡️ Features
* **Kernel Overlay :** High refresh-rate rendering without direct DirectX hook hooks.
* **2D Mini-Radar :** Customizable top-down minimap with real-time enemy positioning.
* **Integrated Triggerbot :** Instant fire on sniper and marksman rifles.
* **Loot & Crate ESP :** High-tier armor satchels, killstreaks, and loadout drop markers.
